Hello! Let’s make some autumn plans together.

📋 Invite · Accept · Refuse

Invite

  1. Let’s fly a kite!
  2. We could go hiking.
  3. How about going shopping?
  4. What about watching the stars?
  5. Would you like to go on an excursion?

Accept

  1. Sounds good!
  2. Why not?
  3. Good idea!
  4. Yes, let’s! I’d love to.

Refuse

  1. I don’t think it’s a good idea.
  2. Sorry, I can’t. I’m busy.
  3. I’d rather stay inside.
  4. Sorry, maybe next time.

Verb forms after the phrases

V (go, fly, do)

  • Let’s go hiking!
  • We could fly a kite.
  • Why don’t we do yoga?
  • Shall we watch TV?

to + V

  • Would you like to go shopping?
  • Do you want to spend the day outside?
  • I’d like to watch the stars.

V-ing

  • How about going to a theme park?
  • What about doing sports?
  • I love flying a kite.
  • I don’t mind doing housework.
