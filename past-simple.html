Past Simple at a glance

Statements — V2

I / you / he / she / we / they + past form

I watched a film. · She went home. · We were happy.

The form is the same for all persons.

Negatives — didn’t + verb

subject + didn’t + first form (no past form after didn’t)

I didn’t watch the film. · She didn’t go home. · It wasn’t cold.

With be we say wasn’t / weren’t, not “didn’t be”.

Questions — Did …?

Did + subject + first form? · Was / Were + subject?

Did you see it? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
Were you at home? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t.

After did the verb never changes: Did you went is wrong.

Wh-questions

question word + did + subject + verb?

Where did you go? · What did she buy? · Why were you late?
But: Who called you? (who = the subject, no did)

where, when, what, who, why, how, what time, how much

Spelling of -ed

work → worked · like → liked · stop → stopped · study → studied · play → played

+e → +d; one vowel + one consonant → double it; consonant + y → ied; vowel + y → +ed.
